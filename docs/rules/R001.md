# R001 — Excessive chunk overlap

**Severity:** warning
**Category:** RAG / chunking sanity

## What this catches

A `[rag]` block whose `chunk_overlap_tokens` is more than **50%** of
`chunk_target_tokens`. The ratio is calculated on the raw numbers; no
normalization for chunking strategy.

## Why it matters

Overlap above 50% means every retrieval chunk duplicates more than
half of its neighbour. Two downstream effects:

- **The reranker spends effort on dedup instead of ranking** —
  it sees the same passage twice from adjacent chunks, and has to
  decide which copy "wins."
- **The index doubles in size** — half the embeddings are encoding
  text that already lives in another chunk. Storage cost goes up
  without a recall payoff.

Typical sweet spot: 10–30% overlap. The default in SPEC §1.4.1
(`chunk_target_tokens = 500`, `chunk_overlap_tokens = 50`) lands at
10% — safe baseline.

## Suggested fix

Reduce `chunk_overlap_tokens` to 15–30% of `chunk_target_tokens`:

```toml
[rag]
chunk_target_tokens = 500
chunk_overlap_tokens = 100   # 20%, well under R001's 50% ceiling
```

If you genuinely need higher overlap (e.g., retrieving very short
spans from very long documents), consider switching to
`chunking_strategy = "header_aware"` and reducing target chunk size
instead.

## When to silence

If your corpus is dominated by short documents where retrieval
quality measurably improves with high overlap (rare), you can suppress
R001 in audit's JSON output. R001 is WARNING so it never breaks CI by
itself.
